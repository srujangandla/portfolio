Network Service Availability Prediction

Epidemiological ML

Disease Outbreak Prediction & Surge Analysis

Problem: Case surges across Indian districts are hard to anticipate.

Solution: Leakage-free temporal splits (train 2009 to 2017, test 2018 to 2019, stress 2020 to 2022) with Random Forest regression and a logistic surge classifier on climate data.

Impact: RMSE 0.95 (R² 0.18 vs 0.00 baseline); surge PR-AUC 0.44 vs 0.22 baseline.

Deep Learning & Wearables

Real-Time Human Activity Recognition & Anomaly Detection

Problem: Cross-dataset distribution shift and data leakage in wearable sensor benchmarks distort real-time activity and fall detection performance.

Solution: Decoupled dual architecture: 1D-CNN for 12-class activity recognition (PAMAP2) and a hybrid 1D-CNN + Conv-Autoencoder with score fusion for fall anomaly detection (SisFall), validated via strict subject-holdout protocols.

Impact: PAMAP2: 91.09% accuracy & 91.23% weighted F1 (1.72 ms/window latency). SisFall: 91.10% accuracy & 0.8723 ROC-AUC, cutting false alarms by over 60%.
